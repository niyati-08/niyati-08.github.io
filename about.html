About

Understanding how RNA structure and dynamics shape biological function through the lens of solution state NMR spectroscopy.

Dr. Niyati Jain

Assistant Professor (Research)  ·  Faculty In charge, NMR Facility  ·  Trivedi School of Biosciences, Ashoka University

Dr. Niyati Jain is an Assistant Professor (Research) at Ashoka University and Faculty In charge of the NMR Facility. She is an RNA structural biologist who uses solution state NMR spectroscopy, together with biochemical and biophysical approaches, to understand how RNA structure and dynamics shape biological function with interests spanning RNA protein interactions, RNA ligand recognition, and non coding RNA structures.

Her research journey has taken her across leading institutions in the United States and India. She earned her Ph.D. with Dr. Tianbing Xia at the University of Texas at Dallas, studying the dynamics of purine sensing riboswitches, and carried out postdoctoral and research work with Dr. Daniel Battle at The Ohio State University and Dr. Blanton Tolbert at Case Western Reserve University, focusing on RNA protein interactions in snRNP assembly and HIV 1 splicing. She has previously held positions as a Ramalingaswami Fellow and Assistant Professor at CSIR IGIB, New Delhi.

Research Interests

RNA Structure & Dynamics

RNA structure and dynamics studied using solution state NMR spectroscopy.

RNA Protein Interactions

RNA protein interactions and their role in post transcriptional gene regulation.

lncRNA & mRNA Biology

Structural biology of long non coding and messenger RNAs, including HOTAIR, HIV, and HCV RNA.

CRISPR Cas9 Systems

Thermodynamics and target specificity of CRISPR Cas9 RNA DNA recognition.

RNA Modifications

The role of m6A modifications in modulating RNA structure and protein recognition.

Solution State NMR

10+ years in RNA focused NMR: multidimensional NMR, isotopic labeling, and 3D structure calculation.
