About the Lab

Understanding how RNA structure and dynamics shape biological function, one molecule at a time.

Dr. Niyati Jain

Assistant Professor (Research)  ·  Faculty In-charge, NMR Facility  ·  Ashoka University

Dr. Niyati Jain leads the JAIN Lab, where the research programme centres on RNA structural biology and solution-state NMR spectroscopy. The lab studies how RNA molecules fold, move, and recognise their partners, and how these properties translate into biological function.

By combining NMR spectroscopy with RNA biochemistry, molecular biology, and a range of biophysical techniques, the lab builds detailed, mechanistic pictures of structured RNAs, RNA–protein complexes, and RNA–ligand recognition.

Our Mission

Discover

Uncover the structures and dynamics of biologically important RNAs that govern gene regulation and disease.

Understand

Connect RNA structure and molecular recognition to function in viral and cellular systems.

Train

Mentor students and researchers in NMR spectroscopy, RNA biochemistry, and rigorous biophysical science.

Our Approach

Research in the JAIN Lab is deeply interdisciplinary. Solution-state NMR spectroscopy sits at the heart of the work, complemented by RNA in vitro transcription and purification, UV thermal melting, circular dichroism, ITC, MST, BLI, EMSA, fluorescence-based assays, and structure–function analysis. Together these tools let the lab study RNA from the level of a single base pair up to complete regulatory mechanisms.
